# Safety

> This is a presence radar, not a medical device and not a safety interlock.

`medical` in the guide manifest is false. Do not use the output to decide care, fall protection, or access control. A cluster id is not a person. A zone flag is not a count of people. A missing frame is `no_source` or an empty target list, which is not proof that a room is empty.

Do not apply 5 V to the module or to the UART pins. The vendor supply range is 3.1 V to 3.5 V. The vendor also states a current of at least 1 A and ripple at most 50 mV. Those are vendor limits. They have not been measured on this board.

The module radiates at 60 GHz, as the vendor states. Follow the vendor's install limits for a occupied room. This cog adds no extra radio transmission. `--command keep` does not transmit a command. The firmware query is one short UART frame, not a radio setting.

OTA type `0x3000` is not sent. Baud-change commands are not sent. Config writes `0x0201` through `0x0207` are documented in the protocol and are not sent.

`--simulate` and any line marked `SYNTHETIC` are generated bytes. `MEASURED` means the bytes came from this process's UART path. The 2026-10-10 axis check used the UART viewer. This cog binary has not been the process on that port.

Keep the export on loopback. The report can describe where a radar thinks objects are. That stays on the machine that owns the sensor.
